ABOUT US

Who We Are

Bringing professionals from across the DMV together to connect, grow, and explore Northern Virginia.

The NOVA Social Club is a curated community bringing professionals from across the DMV together to build meaningful relationships, discover new opportunities, and experience the best of Northern Virginia.

We create spaces where ambitious, like-minded people can connect beyond traditional networking. Through elevated social experiences, professional development, wellness, collaboration, and community service, we make it easier to meet new people, expand your circle, and feel more connected to the region.

Whether you live in Northern Virginia or simply want to experience more of what it has to offer, there is a place for you within our community.

What We Offer

A Community Built Around How You Live, Connect, and Grow.

Wellness and Fitness

Boxing classes, run clubs, group workouts, yoga, outdoor activities, and social wellness experiences that make it easier to prioritize your health while building community.

Explore Northern Virginia

Curated opportunities to discover restaurants, hotels, rooftops, neighborhoods, cultural destinations, and hidden gems across Northern Virginia.

Elevated Social Experiences

From mixers, cocktail socials, dinners, rooftop gatherings, cultural outings, and other memorable experiences designed to help you connect naturally in elevated environments.

Professional Development

Networking events, panels, workshops, conversations, and educational experiences that provide practical insight, introduce new perspectives, and support your professional growth.

Members-Only Perks

Priority access, partner perks, curated introductions, members-only opportunities, and invitations to private experiences across Northern Virginia.

Community First

Volunteer experiences and nonprofit partnerships that give members meaningful ways to support and make a positive impact throughout the region.
